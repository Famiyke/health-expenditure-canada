# Official-source comparison and release vintages

Original CIHI source hub: https://www.cihi.ca/en/national-health-expenditure-trends

The 2025 CIHI release estimated **$398.8 billion in health expenditures for 2025**, in current prices, as a **forecast**. The 2025-edition series showed roughly **$361 billion for 2023**. The subsequent 2026 edition, released November 10, 2026, revised 2023 to approximately **$362 billion** and projects **$433 billion for 2026**. Changing estimates are expected as actuals replace preliminary forecasts and source tables are revised.

2025 source (data in brief): https://www.cihi.ca/sites/default/files/document/health-expenditure-data-in-brief-2025-en.pdf

2026 revised snapshot: https://www.cihi.ca/en/national-health-expenditure-trends/nhex-trends-reports/nhex-trends-2026-snapshot

2026 detailed tables: https://www.cihi.ca/en/national-health-expenditure-trends/nhex-trends-data

These are independent externally verified reference figures and **not outputs of this repository's simulated Python dataset**. The Excel workbook is described as using earlier CIHI NHEX tables. Its cell-by-cell data lineage, category shares and formulas have not been reconciled against the official versioned source workbook.

Do not represent the Python model's provincial rankings, GLP-1 trends, biosimilar spending or potential savings as CIHI findings. For any comparison, specify CIHI edition, reporting year, actual/forecast status, current versus constant dollars, numerator/denominator and any inflation adjustment.

**Validation status:** National top-line expenditure reference independently cross-checked against CIHI publications; project workbook and detailed modeled findings not yet reconciled.
